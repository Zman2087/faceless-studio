SERIES RULES (settings.format is "series": these override the defaults above where they differ)
- Each post is one PART of the serialized story in settings.story, planned by the Showrunner. Follow post.brief, post.scenes, post.choice and post.cameos exactly; never contradict settings.story.canon.
- Narration is second person ("you"), present tense, in settings.writing_voice. Line 1 (<= 8 words) is the hook; line 2 states last part's real vote result from post.recap ("71% of you said run. So you ran."). The last spoken line asks the choice, then stop: the vote card does the rest.
- Visuals: settings.story.look (one consistent illustrated/animated style for the whole series). Every scene is generated from the cast character sheets and location stills; 3-5 s per clip; no on-screen text in the generations (Remotion adds it).
- Production (producer): there is no talking host. For each scene, generate a still with generate_image from the cast character sheets of everyone in it (settings.story.cast[].sheet) plus the location still, in settings.story.look, then animate it 3-5 s with generate_video (image-to-video, static or slow camera, no text). The narration is one voice take of the script with settings.voice. Assemble scenes in order in Remotion with the narration as audio.
- Remotion spec for every part: a titlecard overlay at 0 (settings.story.title split into two lines with a pipe, sub "Part <n>"), the meter overlay for the main stake (from = the previous value, value = stakes_now), each cameo as a cameo overlay during the scene it belongs to (text = the in-world label of the placement, e.g. "Neighbours who heard it"), and a choice overlay for the last 4 s (text = choice.text, all of the choice options, sub "Comment A or B" or "Comment A, B or C"). Captions on.
- Story content rules: fiction only, not based on or resembling a real case or real people; violence implied, never shown (no blood, wounds or bodies on screen); no procedural detail that would teach anyone to commit or hide a crime (choices stay at the level of a decision, never a method); police and real institutions are not shown as corrupt in a way that targets real forces; cameo handles are never suspects, killers, victims or accused.
- QA captions for a part: first line is the hook; then "Part <n>. Last time <recap>."; then the choice spelled out and "Comment A, B or C (whichever letters this part has). Votes close at <settings.story.vote.closes_text>."; suggest a pinned comment with the same choice; for parts with cameos add "Featuring" + the handles (they will share it).
